Artisan Provenance

Made in Galle, shaped by generational hands.

Coconut palms are part of daily life across Sri Lanka. In coastal Galle, our partner woodworking artisans turn discarded fallen shells into functional tea objects of timeless beauty.

Rather than allowing agricultural byproducts to burn, artisans hand-sort mature shells for thickness and density, level the rim, sculpt an ergonomic contrasting handle, and polish the interior with virgin coconut oil.

Behind every cup: Select → Shape → Finish and check
Step 01

Select the shell

Mature coconut shells from local estates are hand-selected for bowl depth, wall thickness, and natural structural density.

Step 02

Shape the cup

The top rim is leveled, hand-sanded across five abrasive grits to a silky touch, and an ergonomic curved hardwood handle is sculpted.

Step 03

Finish and check

Each vessel is cured with organic virgin coconut oil, rigorously inspected for watertight balance, and certified food-safe.

Specifications & Integrity

Details and care

Capacity Approx. 220ml – 250ml (7.5 – 8.5 oz) — ideal volume for standard loose-leaf tea infusions.
Size Approx. 7.5cm Height × 11.5cm Width (including handle); bowl rim diameter ~8.0cm.
Materials Reclaimed natural coconut shell; curved plantation rosewood handle; brass alignment pins; food-safe organic coconut oil seal.
Use Suitable for warm and hot brewed tea up to 85°C (185°F), cold brew infusions, and ceremonial teas. Avoid pouring rolling boiling water (100°C) directly onto an empty, cold shell.
Care Hand wash with mild soap and lukewarm water. Towel dry promptly. Do not microwave, soak, or clean in dishwasher. Periodically rub with a drop of coconut oil to maintain rich luster.
Variation 100% natural organic material: shell color depth, striations, and grain vary naturally from the photograph.
In the box Selected Coconut Shell Tea Cup(s) set, illustrated Ceylon craft provenance card, breathable linen storage bag.
Before your first pour, rinse the cup with warm tap water to gently temper the natural fibers. When steeping Ceylon teas, water at 80°C–85°C preserves delicate top-notes while being ideal for natural shell vessels.
Every few months, or whenever the shell appears dry after repeated washings, apply a tiny drop of food-grade virgin coconut oil or mineral oil using a soft cloth. Allow to absorb for 20 minutes and wipe clean.
