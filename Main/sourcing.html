The Philosophy of Sourcing

The Origin of Taste

Quality begins in the red, mineral-dense soil of Sri Lanka’s southern plains and highland ridges. It lives in unpolluted mountain rainfall, gentle morning mist, and the patient judgment of cultivators who know exactly when a leaf is ready to be plucked and when a bark quill is ready to be shaved.

The name Ceylon has often been diluted by commercial brokers who blend a fraction of island harvest with cheaper teas and spices from other regions. For Graceylon, authentic Ceylon means an uncompromised standard.

Specific details create lasting trust; exaggeration destroys it.
01

100% Sri Lankan Provenance

Every single ingredient and material is grown, harvested, and crafted within Sri Lanka. We never blend across borders.

02

Whole-Leaf Orthodox Integrity

Our teas contain whole and broken-leaf orthodox grades, free from industrial fillers, flavor sprays, or artificial essences.

03

True Alba Cinnamon

We source exclusively Cinnamomum verum. We do not sell cassia, nor do we dilute our quills with secondary bark layers.

04

Sustainable Artisan Materials

Our coconut vessels are shaped from mature fallen coconut shells, utilizing reclaimed resources without felling live trees.

Rigorous Evaluation Protocol

Our Multi-Stage Quality Gateways

Before any harvest batch is accepted into the Graceylon collection, it passes five rigorous sensory and physical evaluations.

01

Botanical Purity & Visual Inspection

Leaves and bark are inspected for color, size uniformity, and structural integrity. For cinnamon, we verify the presence of multi-layered, delicate inner bark quills. For tea, we verify the presence of unbroken leaf structures and golden tips.

02

Aroma & Volatile Oil Density

True Ceylon spices and teas are defined by their natural essential oils. We evaluate batches for fresh, floral, and warm aroma profiles, rejecting any stock that displays dullness or storage age.

03

Cup Liquor & Flavor Clarity

Every tea harvest undergoes cupping across controlled temperatures. We evaluate infusion color, liquor brightness, mouthfeel, and lingering finish. We accept only teas that steep clean, sweet, and balanced without astringency.

04

Craftsmanship & Food-Safe Ergonomics

Each handcrafted coconut vessel is individually inspected for smooth finishing, structural strength, balance in the hand, and proper curing with natural, food-grade coconut oils.

05

Moisture Control & Freshness Sealing

Because Ceylon’s climate is humid, storage is paramount. We dry and seal our harvests within tightly monitored humidity thresholds, locking in freshness before the journey across the oceans begins.

THE SENSORY BENCHMARK · TASTING ARCHITECTURE

Inside the Cupping Room: The Sensory Science of Ceylon Tea

Before any orthodox tea leaves are approved for Graceylon, they undergo strict sommelier cupping. Through standardized porcelain bowls, precision water temperatures, and sensory timing, we evaluate the living character of each single-estate harvest.

Cupping Protocol · Colombo Master Tasters
Testing Method: ISO 3103 Standard Cupping Harvest Sample: Highland Orthodox Leaf

1. Dry Leaf & Bud Structural Assay

Leaves are spread across white cupping trays to inspect tip-to-leaf ratio, velvet down coverage, unbroken bud twist, and moisture equilibrium. Pulverized dust or machine bruising results in instant rejection.

2. Wet Infusion & Volatile Aromatics

Inverted cupping lids capture the first release of steam. We examine the olfactory bouquet for natural elevation markers: mountain wildflower, crushed mint, toasted honeycomb, and clean stone fruit.

3. Liquor Luminescence & Palate Finish

Evaluating cup clarity across white porcelain: liquor brightness, mouthfeel texture, briskness, and clean lingering finish. A true orthodox Ceylon leaf never leaves harsh or bitter astringency.

The Single-Estate Guarantee: Less than 4% of inspected micro-lot auctions meet our threshold for antioxidant concentration and cup sweetness.
FIELD TO VESSEL · ANCESTRAL PEELING CRAFT

The Art That Machines Cannot Replicate

Unlike coarse Cassia bark stripped by industrial machinery, authentic Alba-grade Ceylon cinnamon is created through surgical hand precision passed down across three centuries. Observe how the tender inner bark is transformed into layered golden quills.

Craft Documentary · Galle Groves
Harvest Location: Southern Sri Lanka Estate Grade: Alba (Ø < 6mm)
01

Dawn Branch Selection

Stems of Cinnamomum verum are harvested at early sunrise when morning dew keeps sap and moisture high, allowing the bark to separate cleanly without tearing.

02

Brass Knife Outer Scraping

A master peeler tenderly scrapes away the coarse, bitter outer bark with a traditional brass scraper, revealing the fragrant, parchment-thin inner cambium layer.

03

The Micro-Peeling Ribbon

Using a specialized curved blade, the inner bark is hand-sliced in unbroken, translucent ribbons just fractions of a millimeter thick—a skill demanding years of muscle memory.

04

Telescopic Layering & Shade Cure

Multiple gossamer ribbons are carefully inserted inside one another like a cigar, curled naturally by the tropical air, and shade-dried to lock in essential cinnamaldehyde oils.
