The Origin

Where Passion Meets Heritage

Some places are visited. Others are felt.

Graceylon begins with a feeling: Ceylon deserves to be experienced, not just admired from afar.

The name joins grá, the Gaelic word for love, with Ceylon, the island at the heart of our story. It stands for love of a place and respect for its heritage. It is also a wish to bring a little of that beauty home.

We don't aim to reinvent Ceylon's story. We aim to let you feel it.

"The hush of mist over emerald hills. The warmth of spice carried on the breeze. The quiet pleasure of a cup held between two hands, while the world, for a moment, waits. Graceylon was born from a desire to hold that feeling, and share it."

Before Your First Discovery

Graceylon is an artisanal brand inspired by Ceylon's living heritage. We bring together rare single-origin Ceylon white teas, true cinnamon, selected spices, and handcrafted coconut teaware to turn daily rituals into quiet, meaningful sensory experiences.

Every treasure is sourced directly from Sri Lanka (historically Ceylon). Our teas hail from misty high-elevation cloud forests such as Nuwara Eliya, our true cinnamon comes from traditional southern groves, and our coconut teaware is hand-turned by local artisans. Every product page specifies its exact terroir.

We ship worldwide using premium express couriers with tracked delivery. Orders are packaged in aroma-sealed, protective boxes. Free delivery is available on qualifying tasting flights, and typical international transit takes 3 to 7 business days.

You can contact our curation desk directly at curator@graceylon.com. Whether you have brewing questions, corporate gifting requests, or inquiries regarding your order, our curators personally respond within 24 hours.
