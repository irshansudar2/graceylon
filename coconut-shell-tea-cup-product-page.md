## 3. SEO and Shopify Setup

- **Page title:** Coconut Shell Tea Cup | Handcrafted in Sri Lanka (keep "Handcrafted" only if true)
- **Meta description:** Handcrafted coconut shell tea cup from Sri Lanka, with a curved handle and natural grain. Choose a single cup, pair or gift set. Ships to [region].
- **Headings:** one H1 (product name); H2 per section.
- **Alt text:** describe the image, e.g. "Coconut shell tea cup with curved wooden handle, side view."
- **Structured data:** ensure Product schema (price, availability, reviews); test with Google Rich Results Test.
- **Metafields:** capacity, materials, care, origin, maker story; display via dynamic sources.
- **Speed:** WebP images, first image eager, rest lazy; videos below the buy box.
- **Cross-sell:** "Complete the ritual" row linking to Ceylon tea and related products.

Suggested metafields: custom.capacity_ml, custom.dimensions, custom.materials, custom.interior_finish, custom.origin, custom.maker_story, custom.drink_suitability, custom.care_instructions, custom.package_contents, custom.natural_variation

## 4. Confirm Before Launch

1. Hot-drink suitability and interior finish.
2. Capacity, dimensions, weight.
3. Set contents and prices.
4. Maker/workshop name and photo permission.
5. Delivery times, shipping cost, duties, returns policy.

## 5. First A/B Tests (one at a time)

1. Single-cup photo vs row photo as lead image.
2. Pair pre-selected vs single cup pre-selected.
3. Origin teaser in buy box vs story below only.
4. Sticky mobile Add to cart bar vs none.

Judge on completed orders, then add-to-cart rate and average order value, split by mobile and desktop.
